# How the dashboard's numbers are kept from naming a small group

Moved here from `CLAUDE.md`, where a short version stays. The code is `apps/api/src/metrics`; the tests are `metrics.test.ts`, `metrics-protect.test.ts`, `metrics-leaks.test.ts`, `metrics-crisis.test.ts` and `metrics-outcomes.test.ts`.

(`src/metrics`, `GET /metrics/funnel|timeliness|crisis|equity`, `ops` access only). Every published number goes through `suppress()` (under 10, zero included); a share needs its part, its whole and what is left over to reach 10. Cells that add up to a total (the funnel's source and tier cells, timeliness tiers, the equity groups of every dimension) go through `hiddenCells` (`protect.ts`), which hides cells until no small number can be computed: the hidden cells of a relation must be empty on every count, or hold at least two non-empty cells adding up to ten or more on every count (so a hidden group of 4 with none closed does not publish "0 closed"), and the relations taken together, row-reduced exactly, must pin no non-empty hidden cell (`pinnedCells`). A suppressed cell is `{ suppressed: true }` and nothing else, and every hidden cell shows the same neutral "Suppressed to protect small groups", since many are large and a different label would disclose. The funnel counts journeys (a parentless ticket and its reroutes), cumulative from `TicketEvent` rows, in the root ticket's source and tier; the first stage is open unexpired unclaimed invites plus journeys plus crisis events; loop-closed and the equity rates stay per ticket. The first two stages are one table (`openingTable`), so the first stage minus the second (open invites plus crisis events) is protected, and the crisis figures hide what the funnel hides. Equity's "no bucket given" groups are one table across dimensions: identical ticket sets are tied, sets that differ by 1 to 9 tickets (partial opt-ins) are always hidden together in every dimension they touch, sets that differ by 10 or more are independent. "Derived" means exact recovery of a non-empty hidden cell by a linear combination of the published figures of one response, or one relation exposing a hidden sum under ten. Equity is served for ONE period, the last 90 days (`EQUITY_PERIOD`): `GET /metrics/equity` refuses any other `period` with a 400 and no figures, and the dashboard's period filter does not reach the equity section, which says so, because all time less 90 days is a small count of named people. The window slides with the care clock, so two responses taken on different days (or either side of a demo clock step) can still subtract, and that is accepted. The Loop closed tile reads the same equity figures, so it is always the last 90 days and its note says so. Not covered, by decision: any two published later-stage counts subtract (issued, acknowledged, seen, re-measured), the same funnel or timeliness cell across two periods subtracts, and bounds inferred from counts never being negative. The overdue list shows keyed pseudonymous ids only, at 10 or more overdue.

## Symptom change (the outcomes figures)

`GET /metrics/outcomes` is one more table of the same kind; see `docs/outcome-metrics.md` for what it counts and how it is protected. It reads the funnel's decision for the re-measured stage and hides everything when the funnel hides it.
