# Docs

- [`safe-messaging-checklist.md`](safe-messaging-checklist.md): a self-check of the crisis copy, and the copy still waiting for clinical review.
- [`inbound-screens.md`](inbound-screens.md): how a front door (TAMM, a health centre, a school) signs and sends a positive screen, and what comes back.

- [`demo-e2e-spec.md`](demo-e2e-spec.md): the demo script's end-to-end spec (Gate C), generated from the Playwright spec and its registry by `node scripts/export-demo-spec.mjs`.

- [`product-spec.md`](product-spec.md): the product spec (epics, stories, demo script), exported as handed over, with the decisions taken during the build noted at the top.
- [`running-the-demo.md`](running-the-demo.md): how to turn on `DEMO_MODE` and run the demo.
- [`browser-baselines.md`](browser-baselines.md): how the browser gates' pixel snapshots are made and checked, on macOS and on Linux for CI.

- [`metric-protection.md`](metric-protection.md): how the dashboard's numbers are kept from naming a small group (suppression, complementary hiding, what is not covered).
- [`outcome-metrics.md`](outcome-metrics.md): symptom change among re-measured tickets: the one period it is served for, what it counts and the person's separate opt-in, the reliable-change thresholds and their source, how it is protected, and what the demo shows.
- [`metric-definitions.md`](metric-definitions.md): every dashboard tile's definition, numerator, denominator, period and suppression note in both languages, generated by `pnpm docs:metrics`.
- [`messaging.md`](messaging.md): the Notifier, its mock drivers and keyed Arabic and English templates, and what is never logged.
- [`two-clocks.md`](two-clocks.md): care time, wall time and the demo clock.
- [`anonymous-mode.md`](anonymous-mode.md): what an anonymous session keeps, where, and when it is deleted.

Plans, investigation notes and the design boards live in `.local/` at the workspace root, outside git.
